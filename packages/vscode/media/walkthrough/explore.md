# Explore design directions

Run `/open-design-explore` in Copilot Chat to build 2 to 4 different design directions side by side. Copilot generates several options, each with its own style and approach, so you can compare and choose the best one.

Just describe what you want and ask for several directions, then pick the one to build out.
