# Explore design directions

Run `/open-design-explore` in Copilot Chat to build 2 to 4 different design directions side by side.

Copilot will generate multiple design options, each with its own style and approach, so you can compare and choose the best one.

[Read the guide](https://github.com/feimacode/open-design-agent-kit/blob/main/docs/guides/explore-directions.md)
