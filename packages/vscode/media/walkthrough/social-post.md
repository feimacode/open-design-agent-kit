# Make a social media post

`/open-design-social-post` asks where you're posting (X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Xiaohongshu, Stories and Reels, a YouTube thumbnail or video). It designs at the right size and exports files ready to upload into the artifact's `exports/` folder.

Exporting needs an installed Chrome, Edge or Chromium. Nothing is downloaded.

[Read the guide](https://github.com/feimacode/open-design-agent-kit/blob/main/docs/guides/social-posts.md)
