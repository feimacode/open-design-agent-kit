Add campaign export: channel sizes, fit-to-box text and a campaign sheet

038ac77 → main1 commit44 files+1041 −52

Marketing needs one message in every channel size. This adds nine canvas formats (LinkedIn, Open Graph, headers, an email header at 2×, and four display ads with 150 KB budgets), a data-od-fit attribute that shrinks long translations until they fit, and a campaignSheet export option that shows every shape and every other piece of the campaign on one image.

Risks

  • Default shape sheets. Tiny ads would fail on every poster, so headers, banners and ads are left out of a sheet that names no presets (inDefaultSheet: false).
  • Fluid text. Fitting writes calc((authored size) * k), not frozen pixels, so text still reflows across shapes.

How it was tested

  • campaignKit.test.ts: formats, budgets and the 2× email header; per-row fitting; the campaign sheet with a collection, all in a real browser.
  • Existing poster and export suites (70 tests) unchanged.
